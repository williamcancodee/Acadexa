What Acadexa Is

A simple mission: make study discovery faster, clearer, and more accessible.

Open Source By Design

Acadexa is built as an open-source educational platform. Its code can be improved, audited, and extended by learners, educators, and developers who care about better access to study resources.

How It Helps Learners

You select your level, subjects, and preferred resource types. Acadexa then gathers useful links from trusted online sources and compiles them into a downloadable PDF so your research starts organized.

Built For Continuous Improvement

Because it is open source, Acadexa can grow with new subjects, better filtering, improved accessibility, and richer recommendation logic over time.
